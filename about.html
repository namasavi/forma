A SIMPLE WAY TO THINK ON PAPER

Write it once.
Shape it as you go.

Forma is a lightweight note workspace. The text you write remains your source of truth; the app simply divides it into sections, renders Markdown, and helps you keep each thought easy to scan.

01

Write naturally

Type notes in the left-hand editor. Headings, bullets, bold text, links, and other common Markdown patterns are supported.

02

Use three dashes

Put --- on a line by itself to split your writing into separate blocks. You can edit the original text whenever you want.

03

Keep your work

Your notes autosave in this browser. Use the library to search, open, duplicate, or delete notes. Export Markdown for a portable copy.

NOTION EMBED

Put Forma inside Notion.

Notion cannot embed a website that only runs on your computer. First publish this folder to a public HTTPS host such as GitHub Pages. Then:

  1. Open the Notion page where you want Forma.
  2. Type /embed and choose Embed.
  3. Paste your published Forma website URL, such as the URL ending in /workspace.html.
  4. Resize the embed block to suit your page.

Important: browser-local notes do not automatically sync into Notion or across devices. The embedded page runs as its own website. If you need shared data, Notion database syncing, or accounts, that requires a backend or a separate integration.
